title: Wild West, Baby
date: 19 November 2026
slug: wild-west-baby


Let me be very clear: nobody knows what the fuck is going on right now. Unless a person is literally a product owner, you can take their opinion on the direction a product is headed with the smallest possible pinch of salt. 

We're currently living through the Wild West of product development. People are losing their minds at their ability to create what was previously unimaginable at a speed that was previously impossible. Fabric now has more views than your report has viewers! And in the world of independent vibe coders we're in a period in which if someone decides they don't want to pay for software anymore, they just clone it. Apparently fuck copyright; Wild West, baby!

I don't want to be accused of taking joy in people being wrong, but that doesn't stop it being funny that a certain corner of LinkedIn decided that Power BI Desktop was 'cooked', and twenty-four hours later it got an update that puts it at the centre of one of Fabric's newest and most visible products.

And yet people are still so confident in loudly expressing their opinions on these matters as facts. Product development and coding have become so much faster in such a short period of time that humans are still playing catch-up to the reality that you can no longer see things coming: they're just there. And even worse, it happens so quickly that people can still remember what you said.
